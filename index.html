1. Anatomy of a Fake Sender Address

Attackers use sneaky tricks to make fake emails look like official notifications.

Display Name Spoofing

Anyone can name their email profile "Google Security". Always expand the header to check the actual address behind the name.

Typosquatting

Look closely for lookalike letters: paypaI.com (with a capital I instead of l) or g00gle.com.

Subdomain Tricks

In accounts.google.security-check.com, the real domain is security-check.com, NOT Google!

3. Golden Rules Before You Click

Artificial Urgency

Phishers create panic ("Account suspended in 2 hours!") to force quick action without thinking.

Hover Before Clicking

On desktop, hover your mouse over links to see the real destination URL in the bottom browser bar.

Never Share Credentials

Legitimate companies will never email you asking for your password, PIN, or 2FA codes.

Navigate Directly

When in doubt, open a new tab and manually type the website address instead of clicking links in emails.
